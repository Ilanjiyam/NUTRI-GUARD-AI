Select Age Group

Safety thresholds and health risks vary drastically by age. Tell us who will consume or use this product:

👶 0 – 2 Years

Infant & Toddler

Extremely sensitive organs. High risk from honey, artificial sweeteners, sodium, chemical preservatives, and synthetic dyes.

🧒 3 – 12 Years

Children

Developing nervous and metabolic system. Vulnerable to hyperactivity dyes (Tartrazine), excess sugar, and caffeine.

🧑 13 – 18 Years

Teens & Adolescents

Rapid growth phase. Vulnerable to excessive caffeine/taurine in energy drinks and endocrine disruptors in cosmetics.

🧑‍💼 19 – 59 Years

Adults

Standard adult toxicity levels. Evaluated for trans fats, carcinogens, cardio-metabolic risks, and chemical build-up.

👴 60+ Years

Elderly / Seniors

Heightened risk from sodium (hypertension), harsh preservatives, synthetic sweeteners, and kidney-taxing additives.
